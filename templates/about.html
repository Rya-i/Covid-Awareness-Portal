About COVID-19

What is it?

COVID-19 is an infectious respiratory disease caused by the SARS-CoV-2 virus. Severity ranges from no symptoms to serious illness.

How does it spread?

It spreads mainly through respiratory droplets and aerosols released when an infected person coughs, sneezes, talks or breathes. Close contact and poorly ventilated indoor spaces increase the chance of spread.

Common symptoms

Fever, cough, sore throat, fatigue and loss of taste or smell. See the Symptoms page.

Prevention

Hand hygiene, respiratory etiquette, good ventilation, staying home when ill and vaccination. See Prevention.

Reliable information

Use official sources such as WHO, CDC and India's MoHFW on our Resources page. Guidance changes, so check for updates.
